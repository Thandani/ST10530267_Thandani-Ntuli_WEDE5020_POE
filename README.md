Name: Warm Oven Studio  

* History: Launched in 2017, Warm Oven Studio was established with the purpose of producing quality pastries. They wanted to address the local youth unemployment. They partnered with vocational training centres. The organization created jobs for the local youth to decrease the unemployment rate. Today, Warm Oven Studio operates three urban bakeries. 
 
* Vision: To become the neighbourhoods' most trusted culinary destination, recognized for setting the standard in artisanal excellence and community connection. 

* Mission: To nourish our city through inclusive employment practices and wholesome baking, fostering a space where both our team and our customers can thrive. 

* Target audience: Busy urban professionals, Gen Z and Millennial trend-seekers and any other residents.

Website Goals and Objectives 

Specific Goals: 

* Establish an online storefront allowing customers to view daily menus and pre-order custom pastries. 
* Increase local foot traffic and online direct sales by 25% within six months of launch. 
* Provide essential business details (location, operating hours, services offered). 

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 

* Monthly Traffic: Reach 3,500 unique site visitors per month. 
* Conversion Rate: Achieve a 3% conversion rate on online pre-orders. 
* Bounce Rate: Maintain a bounce rate below 40% through fast loading times and clear navigation.

Key features and functionality  

Core Pages: 

* Home: Hero banner showcasing fresh daily bakes, quick links to order and the business's introduction. 
* About Us: The bakery's origin story, mission, the vision and the team. 
* Product: Categorized products (Pastries, Cakes, Artisanal Breads) with real-time stock status. 
* Enquiry: Dedicated enquiry form for custom celebration cakes and products in the store.  
* Contact Us: Store location, store hours, contact details, and social links. 

Key Functionality: 

* Online pre-order system then pays on delivery or collection.  
* Enquire about anything that involves the store.

 Timeline and Milestones 

Week 1–2: Discovery & Wireframing 
* Finalize sitemap, brand assets, content gathering and wireframes. 

Week 3–4: Frontend & Backend Development 
* Develop responsive HTML and CSS templates, set up database and build menu structure. 

Week 5: Feature Integration 
* Contact forms, location and conduct initial UI testing. 

Week 6: Testing, Quality Assurance & Launch 
* Cross-browser testing, mobile responsiveness checks, performance optimization and site launch.
