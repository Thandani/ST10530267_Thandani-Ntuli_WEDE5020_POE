About Warm Oven Studio

Our History

Launched in 2021, Warm Oven Studio was established with the purpose of producing quality pastries. They wanted to address the local youth unemployment. They partnered with vocational training centres. The organization created jobs for the local youth to decrease the unemployment rate. Today, Warm Oven Studio operates three urban bakeries.

Vision

To become the neighbourhoods' most trusted culinary destination, recognized for setting the standard in artisanal excellence and community connection.

Mission

To nourish our city through inclusive employment practices and wholesome baking, fostering a space where both our team and our customers can thrive.

Angel Rostova

Head Pastry Chef & Co-Founder
